India's Market Regulator Proposes Tax Relief for Foreign Investors

India markets · Reuters · read original ↗

What happened

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is reportedly backing the removal of long-term capital gains (LTCG) tax for foreign portfolio investors (FPIs). This move aims to simplify the tax structure and enhance the attractiveness of Indian capital markets to global institutional capital. The proposal is currently under consideration by the government as part of broader efforts to streamline investment regulations.

India / market angle

If implemented, this would significantly lower the cost of capital for foreign investors, likely leading to increased inflows into Indian equities. It would improve the competitiveness of Indian markets against other emerging market peers and could provide a sustained boost to market liquidity and valuations.

Concerns Raised Over India's Credit-to-GDP Growth Divergence

India economy & policy · NDTV Profit · read original ↗

What happened

Economists have flagged a growing disparity where bank credit growth (at 19%) is significantly outpacing nominal GDP growth (at 10%). There are concerns that this imbalance could fuel inflation and increase systemic risks within the banking sector. Experts are urging the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to implement tighter credit controls to ensure financial stability.

India / market angle

This trend suggests that the RBI may lean toward a more hawkish monetary policy stance to curb excessive credit expansion. For investors, this implies potential volatility in banking stocks and a higher-for-longer interest rate environment, which impacts borrowing costs for Indian corporates.

What to watch

The next RBI Monetary Policy Committee meeting minutes and any specific regulatory circulars targeting unsecured retail credit growth.
