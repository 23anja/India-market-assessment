Indian Equity Markets Face Sustained Pressure Amid Rising Oil Prices and Bond Yields

India markets · reuters.com · read original ↗

What happened

Indian stock indices, specifically the Sensex and Nifty, have faced a prolonged downward trend, struggling under the weight of elevated global crude oil prices and rising domestic bond yields. These macroeconomic headwinds have dampened investor sentiment, leading to a period of sustained volatility and underperformance compared to earlier market highs. The sell-off reflects broader concerns about corporate profitability in a high-cost environment.

India / market angle

As a major net importer of crude oil, India's current account deficit and inflation outlook are highly sensitive to oil prices; higher costs pressure the INR and force the RBI to maintain tighter monetary policy. Simultaneously, rising bond yields increase the cost of capital for Indian firms and make equity investments less attractive relative to fixed-income assets, directly impacting FII flows and Nifty valuations.

What to watch

Monitor the upcoming RBI Monetary Policy Committee meeting and monthly trade deficit data for signals on how the central bank plans to balance growth with currency stability.

ASML Warns of Impact from Intensified US-China Chip Export Restrictions

China & global trade · simplywall.st · read original ↗

What happened

ASML, the critical Dutch manufacturer of semiconductor lithography equipment, has issued a public warning regarding the tightening of US-led export controls on China. These restrictions aim to limit China's access to advanced chip-making technology, citing national security concerns, but they also complicate global supply chain stability for semiconductor manufacturers reliant on the Chinese market.

India / market angle

India is currently positioning itself as a global semiconductor hub through the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM). While the US-China tech war creates supply chain friction, it also presents an opportunity for India to attract manufacturing capacity that is diversifying away from China. However, Indian firms importing high-end equipment may face longer lead times or increased compliance scrutiny due to the global tech-decoupling.

What to watch

Watch for new US Department of Commerce export control updates and any shifts in capital expenditure plans by major semiconductor foundries looking to establish facilities in India.

YouTube Creator Ecosystem's Significant Contribution to India's GDP

India economy & policy · MediaNews4U · read original ↗

What happened

A new report highlights that YouTube's creative ecosystem contributed over ₹18,000 crore to India's GDP in 2025, while supporting nearly one million jobs. This underscores the rapid formalization and economic scale of the digital creator economy, which has become a substantial driver of employment and entrepreneurial activity in the country.

India / market angle

This growth illustrates a structural shift in the Indian labor market, where digital platforms are facilitating income generation outside traditional corporate sectors. For investors and policymakers, this represents a maturing 'creator economy' sector that influences domestic consumption patterns, digital advertising spends, and the growth of ancillary services like fintech and e-commerce.

What to watch

Look for future government policy frameworks regarding the regulation and taxation of digital content creators and the potential for increased institutional investment in creator-led startups.
