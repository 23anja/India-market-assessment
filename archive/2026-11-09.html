China & global trade · Rare Earth Exchanges · read original ↗

What happened

China has announced new export control measures on specific rare earth elements, a move viewed as a strategic maneuver in the ongoing global competition for critical minerals. These materials are essential for high-tech manufacturing, including semiconductors, electric vehicle batteries, and defense equipment. The restrictions are expected to tighten global supply chains and increase costs for manufacturers reliant on Chinese processing capacity.

India / market angle

As India aggressively scales its manufacturing sector under the 'Make in India' initiative and seeks to build a domestic semiconductor and EV ecosystem, it is currently dependent on imports for critical minerals. These controls highlight the urgency for India to secure alternative supply chains or accelerate the exploration and processing of its own mineral reserves to avoid production bottlenecks.

What to watch

Watch for updates on the specific list of minerals affected and any reciprocal trade policy announcements from the U.S. or EU that might impact global pricing.
