RBI Maintains Repo Rate Amidst 6.9% GDP Growth Projection

What happened

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to keep the repo rate unchanged, prioritizing price stability while maintaining a positive outlook on economic expansion. The central bank projects real GDP growth for the current fiscal year to reach 6.9%, signaling confidence in domestic consumption and investment despite global headwinds.

India / market angle

This decision directly influences borrowing costs for Indian households and corporations, impacting credit growth and liquidity in the banking system. It also serves as a signal to foreign institutional investors regarding the RBI's commitment to containing inflation versus supporting growth, which affects INR stability and capital inflows.

US and China Intensify Strategic Competition Over Critical Minerals

China & global trade · Reuters · read original ↗

What happened

The United States is increasing investments in domestic and allied critical mineral supply chains to reduce reliance on Chinese processing. China, meanwhile, continues to leverage its dominant position in rare-earth elements through export controls and strategic trade restrictions, creating a volatile landscape for global manufacturing.

India / market angle

As India seeks to scale its electronics and green energy manufacturing sectors (e.g., PLI schemes), it is highly dependent on imported critical minerals. The US-China rivalry forces India to accelerate its own mineral security strategy, including securing mining assets abroad and developing domestic processing capabilities to avoid supply chain disruptions.

What to watch

Progress on the India-US partnership under the Minerals Security Partnership (MSP) and any new Indian government policies regarding domestic rare-earth mineral extraction.
