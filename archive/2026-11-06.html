Indian Equities Outperform Amid Global Market Volatility

India markets · Reuters · read original ↗

What happened

Indian stock markets have demonstrated resilience and strong gains, decoupling from the broader sell-off seen in other Asian and global markets. This performance is largely attributed to a decline in global oil prices, which benefits India's import-heavy economy, alongside sustained foreign institutional investor (FII) inflows.

India / market angle

Lower oil prices reduce India's import bill, narrowing the current account deficit and easing inflationary pressures, which provides room for the RBI to maintain a balanced monetary policy. Sustained FII inflows support the INR and provide liquidity to domestic capital markets, insulating them from the volatility currently affecting tech-heavy global indices.

What to watch

Monitor the upcoming Brent crude price trends and FII net buying/selling data in the next two weeks to see if the decoupling from global markets is sustainable.

Economic Impact of Air Pollution in India

India economy & policy · NDTV · read original ↗

What happened

A new report highlights that air pollution is costing India up to 9.5% of its annual GDP, with approximately 2 million deaths linked to poor air quality. The findings advocate for a shift toward health-led economic policies to mitigate these significant human and financial losses.

India / market angle

The loss of 9.5% of GDP represents a massive drag on productivity and public health expenditure. For investors and policymakers, this underscores the long-term fiscal risk of environmental degradation and the potential for increased regulatory costs or mandatory green transition requirements for Indian industries.

What to watch

Watch for potential government policy shifts or budgetary allocations toward green infrastructure and pollution control measures in the upcoming Union Budget.

China's Export Restrictions on Tungsten Impact AI Supply Chains

China & global trade · Nikkei Asia · read original ↗

What happened

China has implemented export curbs on tungsten, a critical mineral used in industrial drill bits and high-tech manufacturing, including components essential for AI hardware. These restrictions are causing supply chain strain for global technology firms that rely on Chinese-processed minerals.

India / market angle

As India seeks to scale its semiconductor and high-end manufacturing ecosystem (e.g., under the PLI schemes), reliance on a concentrated supply chain for critical minerals like tungsten poses a strategic vulnerability. Increased global prices for these materials could raise input costs for Indian hardware manufacturers and defense contractors.

What to watch

Monitor the progress of India’s Critical Minerals Mission and any new bilateral trade agreements aimed at securing non-Chinese sources for rare earth and strategic minerals.
