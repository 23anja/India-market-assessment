What happened

The Reserve Bank of India has opted to keep the repo rate unchanged, prioritizing price stability as it monitors inflationary pressures. Simultaneously, the central bank has projected a real GDP growth rate of 6.9% for the current fiscal year, reflecting a balanced outlook on domestic economic resilience.

India / market angle

The decision to hold rates provides stability for corporate borrowing costs and mortgage rates in India. For equity markets, this signals a focus on maintaining liquidity and supporting growth, though the lack of a rate cut may disappoint investors hoping for an immediate boost to consumption-heavy sectors.

What to watch

Watch the upcoming CPI (Consumer Price Index) inflation prints and the RBI's commentary on food inflation, which remain the primary variables that could force a shift in the current 'neutral' stance.

China Reports Record Trade Surplus Driven by Tech and Auto Exports

China & global trade · easternherald.com · read original ↗

What happened

China's trade surplus reached $119 billion in August, bolstered by a 130% surge in semiconductor exports and a 43% rise in automotive shipments. This data highlights China's aggressive push into high-value manufacturing as it seeks to offset weak domestic consumption through global market expansion.

India / market angle

The surge in Chinese exports, particularly in semiconductors and autos, poses a direct competitive threat to India's 'Make in India' ambitions. As China floods global markets with these goods, Indian manufacturers face increased price competition both domestically and in third-party export markets, potentially widening India's trade deficit with China if domestic demand for these imports remains high.

What to watch

Watch for potential anti-dumping investigations or new tariff measures from the Indian government targeting Chinese industrial components and finished goods to protect local nascent manufacturing.
