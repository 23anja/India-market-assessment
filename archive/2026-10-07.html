RBI Hikes Repo Rate by 25 Basis Points to 5.50%

Global economy & central banks · The Indian Express · read original ↗

What happened

The Reserve Bank of India has increased the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% in a move to combat inflationary pressures. Alongside the hike, the central bank revised its GDP growth forecast for FY27 upward to 7.1%, citing strong domestic economic resilience despite global headwinds.

India / market angle

This policy tightening directly increases borrowing costs for businesses and retail consumers, likely leading to higher EMIs on home and auto loans. While it aims to stabilize the INR and curb inflation, it may dampen short-term credit growth and impact the valuation of interest-rate-sensitive sectors like banking and real estate.

What to watch

Future RBI Monetary Policy Committee meeting minutes for clues on whether this is the start of a sustained hiking cycle or a one-off adjustment.

India Proposes Three-Part Truce in Russia-Ukraine War

Global geopolitics · Hindustan Times · read original ↗

What happened

India has reportedly proposed a three-part framework to de-escalate the Russia-Ukraine conflict, focusing on maintaining the stability of global energy and food supply chains. The initiative seeks to ensure the uninterrupted flow of grain and energy resources, which have been severely disrupted by the ongoing hostilities.

India / market angle

As a major importer of crude oil and fertilizers, India is highly vulnerable to supply chain shocks caused by the war. A successful diplomatic intervention that secures energy corridors would help stabilize India's import bill, reduce volatility in domestic fuel prices, and mitigate inflationary pressures on the economy.

What to watch

The response from both Moscow and Kyiv to the proposal and any subsequent diplomatic engagement at upcoming G20 or UN forums.

Ongoing Impact of China's Export Controls on Critical Minerals

China & global trade · JDSupra · read original ↗

What happened

Nine months after China implemented strict export controls on critical minerals like gallium and germanium, global supply chains remain under significant stress. These minerals are essential for high-tech manufacturing, including semiconductors, telecommunications equipment, and defense technologies.

India / market angle

India's 'Make in India' initiative in electronics and semiconductor manufacturing relies on the availability of these specific minerals. Prolonged Chinese export restrictions force Indian manufacturers to seek alternative, often more expensive, supply sources or accelerate domestic mining and refining capabilities, directly impacting the cost structure of India's tech hardware sector.

What to watch

Updates on India's bilateral agreements for critical mineral sourcing, particularly with partners like Australia or the US, to diversify supply chains away from China.
