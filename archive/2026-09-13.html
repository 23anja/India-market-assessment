Global institutional investors reduce exposure to Indian equities

India markets · The Economic Times · read original ↗

What happened

Recent reports indicate that some global funds are souring on Indian stocks, with several institutions opting to reduce their allocations, in some cases to zero. This shift reflects a reassessment of valuation premiums in the Indian market compared to other emerging markets and broader global macroeconomic concerns.

India / market angle

This trend directly impacts the Indian capital markets, potentially leading to increased volatility and downward pressure on benchmark indices like the Nifty and Sensex. For the Indian economy, a sustained outflow of foreign institutional investor (FII) capital could tighten liquidity and increase the cost of capital for domestic firms reliant on equity financing.

What to watch

Monitor the monthly NSDL data on FII flows and the upcoming quarterly earnings season to see if domestic institutional investors (DIIs) can absorb the selling pressure.

ASEAN surpasses China as top semiconductor supply chain hub for the US

China & global trade · finance.biggo.com · read original ↗

What happened

Data reveals that ASEAN nations have overtaken China as the primary route for South Korean semiconductor exports destined for the United States. This shift highlights the ongoing 'de-risking' of supply chains as manufacturers move assembly and testing operations to Southeast Asia to bypass trade restrictions and geopolitical friction between Washington and Beijing.

India / market angle

This shift presents a significant opportunity for India's 'China Plus One' strategy. As global electronics supply chains diversify away from China, India is actively competing to attract semiconductor assembly, testing, and packaging (OSAT) facilities to integrate into these new, more resilient regional supply networks.

What to watch

Watch for new announcements regarding semiconductor manufacturing incentives or partnerships under the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) that specifically target firms currently shifting operations out of China.
