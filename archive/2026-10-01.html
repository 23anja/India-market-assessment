Indian Equities Face Significant Outflows Amid Global Market Volatility

India markets · Reuters · read original ↗

What happened

Indian benchmark indices have recorded their worst monthly performance since March, driven by a combination of persistent foreign institutional investor (FII) selling and global macroeconomic pressures. The market decline has been exacerbated by concerns over rising oil prices and the impact of global interest rate environments on emerging market capital flows. This trend marks a notable shift in sentiment as domestic funds struggle to fully offset the sustained exit of foreign capital.

India / market angle

The sustained FII outflow directly impacts the INR-USD exchange rate, putting pressure on the rupee. Furthermore, it tightens liquidity in the Indian financial system and forces a repricing of equity valuations, which complicates capital raising for Indian corporates and impacts retail investor sentiment.

What to watch

Monitor the upcoming FII net flow data and the RBI's intervention strategy in the currency markets to stabilize the rupee against the strengthening dollar.

RBI Maintains Repo Rate Amid Growth Projections

India economy & policy · News On AIR · read original ↗

What happened

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has decided to keep the repo rate unchanged, prioritizing inflation control while maintaining a growth-supportive stance. The central bank projects India's real GDP growth for the current fiscal year at 6.9%, signaling a cautious but optimistic outlook on domestic economic resilience.

India / market angle

The decision to hold rates provides stability for borrowing costs across the economy, directly benefiting the real estate and automotive sectors. It also signals that the RBI is prioritizing domestic price stability over following global central bank rate trends, which helps maintain the attractiveness of Indian debt markets for domestic investors.

What to watch

Watch for the next Consumer Price Index (CPI) print to see if inflation remains within the RBI's comfort zone, which will dictate the timing of any future rate pivots.

EU Moves Toward Trade Defense Mechanisms Against China

China & global trade · finance.biggo.com · read original ↗

What happened

France and Germany are advocating for the European Union to adopt a 'Section 301'-style trade mechanism, similar to the U.S. approach, to pressure China over unfair trade practices. Chinese industry groups have formally opposed this move, warning of potential retaliatory measures that could escalate trade tensions between the two major economic blocs.

India / market angle

As the EU seeks to diversify its supply chains away from China, India has a strategic opportunity to position itself as an alternative manufacturing hub. However, if the EU imposes broader protectionist trade barriers, it could inadvertently affect Indian exporters who rely on European markets for finished goods that may contain Chinese-origin components.

What to watch

Monitor the formal legislative progress of the EU's proposed trade defense instruments and any subsequent announcements from the Indian Ministry of Commerce regarding export opportunities in European markets.
