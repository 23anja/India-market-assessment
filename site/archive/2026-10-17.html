China Announces New Export Controls on AI, Biotech, and Solar Technologies

China & global trade · Nikkei Asia · read original ↗

What happened

China has unveiled plans to impose stricter export controls on a wide range of advanced technologies, including artificial intelligence, biopharmaceuticals, and solar manufacturing equipment, citing national security concerns. This move is widely viewed as a strategic counter-measure to tightening Western restrictions on Chinese access to high-end semiconductors and other critical tech. The controls will require exporters to obtain government approval for shipping these technologies abroad.

India / market angle

India is aggressively pursuing a 'China Plus One' strategy and massive domestic solar manufacturing expansion through PLI schemes. China's control over solar technology exports could create supply chain bottlenecks for Indian firms that rely on Chinese equipment or intellectual property. Conversely, if these controls hinder Chinese dominance in the global market, it may accelerate India's efforts to achieve self-reliance in high-tech manufacturing and clean energy infrastructure.

What to watch

Monitor the specific list of technologies subject to the new licensing requirements and whether Indian solar manufacturers report delays in importing essential production machinery.

US Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady Amid Persistent Inflation

Global economy & central banks · ABC News - Breaking News, Latest News and Videos · read original ↗

What happened

The US Federal Reserve has maintained current interest rates as recent data indicates inflation remains at a three-year high, complicating the path toward monetary easing. Policymakers are balancing the risk of premature rate cuts against the potential for economic cooling, leading to a 'higher-for-longer' interest rate environment. This decision reflects ongoing uncertainty regarding the trajectory of US consumer prices.

India / market angle

A 'higher-for-longer' US interest rate environment puts sustained pressure on the Indian Rupee (INR) as the interest rate differential favors the US Dollar, potentially leading to capital outflows from Indian equity markets. Furthermore, it limits the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) flexibility to cut domestic rates, as the central bank must maintain a spread to prevent excessive currency depreciation and imported inflation.

What to watch

The upcoming US Consumer Price Index (CPI) reports and the Federal Reserve's next meeting minutes for any shift in the 'dot plot' projections regarding future rate cuts.

IMF Upgrades India's GDP Growth Forecast to 7% for 2026-27

India economy & policy · News On AIR · read original ↗

What happened

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has revised its growth outlook for India, projecting a 7% GDP expansion for the 2026-27 fiscal year. This optimistic forecast is supported by expectations of resilient domestic demand, improved private investment cycles, and ongoing structural reforms. The revision places India among the fastest-growing major economies globally.

India / market angle

This upward revision serves as a positive signal for foreign institutional investors (FIIs), potentially increasing capital inflows into Indian markets. For domestic policymakers, it reinforces the narrative of India as a stable growth engine, which is critical for attracting long-term foreign direct investment (FDI) in manufacturing and infrastructure sectors.

What to watch

Quarterly GDP data releases and private capital expenditure (Capex) figures from major Indian corporations to see if they align with the IMF's optimistic investment projections.
