Indian Markets Experience Significant Correction Amid Global Volatility

India markets · The Economic Times · read original ↗

What happened

The Sensex and Nifty 50 indices faced a sharp decline, with the Nifty dropping below 23,150, wiping out approximately Rs 9 lakh crore in investor wealth. The sell-off was driven by a combination of elevated global oil prices, rising bond yields, and broader concerns over market valuations.

India / market angle

The correction directly impacts domestic retail and institutional investor sentiment and portfolio valuations. Elevated oil prices increase India's import bill, putting pressure on the Current Account Deficit (CAD) and the INR, while rising bond yields increase borrowing costs for Indian corporates.

What to watch

Monitor the next RBI monetary policy committee meeting and upcoming FII (Foreign Institutional Investor) flow data to see if the selling pressure is structural or a temporary liquidity adjustment.

Bank of Japan Poised to Raise Interest Rates to 31-Year High

Global economy & central banks · Reuters · read original ↗

What happened

The Bank of Japan (BOJ) is signaling an imminent interest rate hike to combat persistent inflation, aiming for levels not seen in over three decades. This move marks a significant departure from the country's long-standing ultra-loose monetary policy.

India / market angle

Japan is a major source of 'carry trade' capital. A rate hike strengthens the Yen, potentially forcing a reversal of these trades where investors borrow cheap Yen to invest in higher-yielding assets, including Indian equities and debt, which could lead to increased volatility in Indian capital markets.

What to watch

The official BOJ policy announcement and the subsequent movement in the USD/JPY exchange rate, which dictates the pace of global capital repatriation.

China & global trade · NDTV Profit · read original ↗

What happened

Indian commerce officials have held discussions with Chinese counterparts, specifically targeting the significant trade deficit between the two nations. The talks aim to address market access barriers and structural imbalances that have persisted despite high volumes of bilateral trade.

India / market angle

China remains a critical source of industrial inputs and electronics for India. Reducing the trade imbalance is a strategic priority for India to lower its import dependency and protect domestic manufacturing sectors, particularly in chemicals and capital goods.

What to watch

Any concrete policy announcements regarding tariff adjustments or non-tariff barrier removals following these diplomatic engagements.
