Nvidia CEO: US Export Curbs Accelerating China's Chip Development

China & global trade · CNBC TV18 · read original ↗

What happened

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated that US export restrictions on advanced semiconductors have forced China to accelerate its domestic chip manufacturing capabilities. By limiting access to high-end GPUs, the US policy has incentivized Chinese tech firms to invest heavily in self-sufficiency and alternative architectures. This shift suggests that while short-term access is restricted, China's long-term reliance on Western silicon may diminish.

India / market angle

India is currently attempting to build its own semiconductor ecosystem through the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM). China’s accelerated push for domestic self-reliance creates a dual-edged sword: it intensifies global competition for mature-node manufacturing equipment and talent, but also provides India an opportunity to position itself as a 'China+1' manufacturing alternative for global firms seeking to diversify their supply chains away from Chinese-controlled tech.

What to watch

Future US Department of Commerce updates on export control lists and the progress of India’s semiconductor fabrication plants (fabs) currently under construction.

Rising Global Crude Oil Prices Weigh on Indian Equity Markets

India markets · Reuters · read original ↗

What happened

Global crude oil prices have surged, leading to renewed concerns about persistent inflation and higher interest rates. The rise in energy costs has triggered a sell-off in equity markets, as investors fear that input costs for manufacturers will compress margins and dampen consumer spending. This trend is being felt globally, but is particularly acute in emerging markets that are net importers of energy.

India / market angle

India imports over 80% of its crude oil requirements; higher prices directly inflate the country's import bill, widen the Current Account Deficit (CAD), and put downward pressure on the Indian Rupee (INR). Furthermore, sustained high oil prices force the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to maintain a more hawkish monetary policy to combat imported inflation, which increases borrowing costs for Indian corporates and consumers.

What to watch

Weekly inventory data from the US Energy Information Administration (EIA) and any production policy shifts from the OPEC+ alliance.

China’s Export Restrictions on Yttrium Raise Global Supply Chain Risks

China & global trade · ChemAnalyst · read original ↗

What happened

China has implemented new export restrictions on yttrium, a critical rare earth element essential for high-tech manufacturing, including defense, electronics, and green energy technologies. These controls are viewed as a strategic maneuver to leverage China's dominance in the rare earth supply chain amidst ongoing trade tensions with Western nations. The move disrupts global supply chains that are heavily dependent on Chinese processing capacity.

India / market angle

India possesses significant rare earth deposits but currently lacks the sophisticated processing infrastructure to compete with China’s refined output. These restrictions highlight the vulnerability of India’s electronics and defense manufacturing sectors, which rely on imported critical minerals. It underscores the urgency for India to accelerate its 'Critical Minerals' strategy and seek strategic partnerships with countries like Australia or Japan to secure non-Chinese supply lines.

What to watch

Developments in the India-Australia Critical Minerals Investment Partnership and any new domestic mining auctions for rare earth elements in India.
