World Bank Upgrades India's FY27 GDP Growth Forecast to 7.1%

India economy & policy · The Indian Express · read original ↗

What happened

The World Bank has revised its growth projection for India's FY27 fiscal year to 7.1%, citing strong economic momentum and resilience. This forecast positions India as a significant outlier in regional growth, outpacing many of its South Asian and global peers.

India / market angle

This upgrade reinforces investor confidence in the Indian macro story, likely supporting foreign institutional investment (FII) inflows into equity markets. It also provides the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) with more policy flexibility, as a robust growth outlook allows them to maintain a hawkish stance on inflation without fearing an immediate recessionary impact.

What to watch

Upcoming Q2 corporate earnings reports and RBI's next monetary policy committee meeting to see if domestic inflation data aligns with this optimistic growth trajectory.

Ukraine Claims Destruction of Half of Russia's Oil Refining Capacity

Global geopolitics · theguardian.com · read original ↗

What happened

Kyiv has reported significant success in targeting Russian oil infrastructure, claiming that approximately 50% of the nation's refining capacity has been neutralized through drone strikes. This development marks a strategic escalation in the economic warfare aspect of the ongoing conflict.

India / market angle

India is a major buyer of Russian crude oil, which is refined and sold globally. A sustained reduction in Russian refining capacity could shift the global supply mix from refined products to crude, potentially tightening global fuel supplies and increasing price volatility for India's energy import bill, which directly impacts the current account deficit and the INR.

What to watch

Global refined product crack spreads and any changes in the discount levels at which India purchases Russian crude oil.

Fed Faces Policy Dilemma as AI Boom Defies High Interest Rates

Global economy & central banks · The New York Times · read original ↗

What happened

The U.S. Federal Reserve is struggling to cool the economy as the artificial intelligence investment boom continues to drive capital expenditure and market optimism despite elevated interest rates. This persistence of growth in the tech sector complicates the Fed's efforts to reach its 2% inflation target.

India / market angle

As the U.S. remains the primary driver of global liquidity, the 'higher-for-longer' interest rate environment necessitated by this resilience keeps the U.S. dollar strong and yields high. This exerts downward pressure on the Indian Rupee and limits the RBI's ability to cut domestic rates, as the central bank must maintain a spread to prevent capital flight from Indian debt markets.

What to watch

U.S. CPI inflation data and upcoming Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting minutes for signals on future rate path adjustments.
