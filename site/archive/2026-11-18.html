India Considers Tax Adjustments to Curb Excessive Derivatives Trading

India markets · Bloomberg.com · read original ↗

What happened

The Indian government is exploring tax policy changes aimed at cooling down the retail-driven speculative surge in the domestic equity derivatives market. These measures are intended to shift investor focus toward long-term capital formation rather than high-frequency, short-term betting.

India / market angle

This directly impacts retail participation and liquidity in the NSE/BSE. If implemented, it could reduce trading volumes for brokerage firms, alter market volatility profiles, and potentially redirect capital flows toward cash-market equities or mutual funds.

What to watch

Any formal notification from the Ministry of Finance regarding changes to the Securities Transaction Tax (STT) or capital gains treatment for F&O trades.

ICRA Projects India's Q2 GDP Growth to Moderate to 7.5%

India economy & policy · PSU Watch · read original ↗

What happened

Rating agency ICRA forecasts India's GDP growth for the July-September quarter to ease to 7.5%, marking a five-quarter low. The slowdown is attributed to a combination of weakening domestic demand and the impact of a patchy monsoon season on agricultural and rural consumption.

India / market angle

This signals a potential cooling in the economy that could influence the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) stance on interest rates. A sustained slowdown may pressure corporate earnings in consumption-linked sectors and impact foreign institutional investor (FII) sentiment regarding India's growth premium.

What to watch

The official GDP data release from the National Statistical Office (NSO) to confirm if the slowdown aligns with these projections.

Indian Drone Manufacturers Accelerate Supply Chain Localization

China & global trade · Business Standard · read original ↗

What happened

Indian drone companies are actively seeking to reduce reliance on Chinese components by indigenizing their supply chains. This shift is driven by both government mandates to secure critical infrastructure and the need to mitigate risks from potential future export restrictions.

India / market angle

This is a significant structural shift for the Indian defense and tech manufacturing sectors. It creates opportunities for domestic component suppliers, reduces the trade deficit in high-tech electronics, and aligns with India's 'Atmanirbhar Bharat' (Self-reliant India) strategic defense goals.

What to watch

Government announcements regarding further import restrictions on drone components or new production-linked incentive (PLI) schemes for domestic drone hardware.
