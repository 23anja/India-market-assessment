Zelenskyy Links Ukraine War Resolution to India's Oil Trade with Russia

Global geopolitics · Hindustan Times · read original ↗

What happened

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has suggested that India's continued purchase of Russian oil is a critical factor sustaining Moscow's war effort. He argued that if India were to cease these energy imports, it would significantly constrain Russia's financial capacity to continue the conflict.

India / market angle

This highlights the growing diplomatic pressure on New Delhi to balance its strategic autonomy and energy security needs against Western and Ukrainian expectations. India currently benefits from discounted Russian crude, which helps manage its domestic inflation and current account deficit; any shift in this policy would directly impact India's energy import bill and macroeconomic stability.

What to watch

Future diplomatic engagements between New Delhi and Kyiv, and any potential shifts in India's energy procurement strategy as global pressure mounts.

S&P Global Upgrades India's FY27 GDP Growth Forecast to 7%

India economy & policy · NDTV · read original ↗

What happened

S&P Global Ratings has revised its GDP growth projection for India for the 2026-27 fiscal year upward to 7%, citing a strengthening economic outlook. The agency also anticipates that the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) may implement a 25-basis-point rate hike in the near term to manage inflationary pressures.

India / market angle

This upgrade signals strong foreign investor confidence in India's medium-term growth trajectory, which typically supports FPI inflows into Indian equity markets. However, the projected RBI rate hike suggests a tightening interest rate environment, which could increase borrowing costs for Indian corporates and impact interest-rate-sensitive sectors like real estate and banking.

What to watch

The upcoming RBI Monetary Policy Committee meeting minutes and domestic CPI inflation data for cues on the timing of a potential rate hike.
