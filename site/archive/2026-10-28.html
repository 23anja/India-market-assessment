US Federal Reserve Expected to Maintain Current Interest Rates

What happened

The Federal Reserve is widely expected to keep interest rates steady in its upcoming decision, despite some economic indicators suggesting a potential case for rate hikes. Analysts believe the central bank will prioritize stability ahead of the U.S. midterm elections, balancing persistent inflation concerns against broader economic performance. This decision reflects a cautious approach to monetary policy in a volatile political and macroeconomic environment.

India / market angle

For India, a 'hold' by the Fed is generally positive as it reduces pressure on the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to aggressively hike domestic rates to defend the rupee. Stable US rates help curb capital outflows from Indian equity markets, which are sensitive to the interest rate differential between the US and emerging markets, and assist in stabilizing the INR-USD exchange rate.

Need for Solar Manufacturing Incentives Amid China's Export Restrictions

China & global trade · Construction World India · read original ↗

What happened

Industry experts are calling for enhanced government incentives to boost domestic solar manufacturing in India to mitigate the impact of China's tightening export controls on critical solar components. As China exerts greater control over its supply chains and technology transfers, India faces risks to its ambitious renewable energy targets. Expanding local production capacity is seen as a necessary step to ensure energy security and reduce reliance on foreign suppliers.

India / market angle

This directly affects India's energy transition strategy and the viability of large-scale solar projects. If India fails to build a robust domestic supply chain, it remains vulnerable to price volatility and supply disruptions from China, which could inflate project costs and delay the country's net-zero commitments.

What to watch

Potential updates to the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes for solar modules and any new trade policy measures targeting renewable energy imports.
