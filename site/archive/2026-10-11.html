What happened

The data suggests that India continues to be a significant outlier in terms of growth momentum among major global economies.

India Seeks 'Critical Mineral' Status for Mica to Reduce Dependence on China

China & global trade · Siliconindia · read original ↗

What happened

The Indian government is considering designating mica as a critical mineral to bolster domestic supply chains and reduce reliance on Chinese market dominance. Mica is essential for high-tech electronics, electric vehicle batteries, and defense applications. This move is part of a broader strategic effort to secure raw material self-sufficiency amid global supply chain fragmentation.

India / market angle

This policy shift directly benefits Indian mining and manufacturing sectors by providing state support and incentives for domestic processing. Strategically, it aligns with India's 'Make in India' initiative and its goal to become a global hub for electronics and EV manufacturing, reducing vulnerability to geopolitical leverage from China.

What to watch

Formal notification of the 'critical mineral' status and any accompanying government incentives or regulatory changes for the mining sector.
