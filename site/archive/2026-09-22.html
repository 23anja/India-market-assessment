India's Trade Deficit with China Widens Despite Export Growth

China & global trade · Business Standard · read original ↗

What happened

While India has seen a notable 40% increase in exports to China, the overall trade deficit continues to expand due to a significantly higher volume of Chinese imports. The surge in imports is largely driven by demand for industrial inputs and electronics, highlighting a persistent structural imbalance in bilateral trade.

India / market angle

The widening deficit puts pressure on the INR and complicates India's 'Make in India' objectives, as domestic manufacturers struggle to compete with low-cost Chinese imports. It also raises strategic concerns regarding supply chain dependencies in critical sectors like pharmaceuticals and electronics.

What to watch

Updates on potential non-tariff barriers or anti-dumping investigations by the Indian government on specific Chinese industrial components.
