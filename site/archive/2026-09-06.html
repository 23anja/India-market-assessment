What happened

India's economy grew by 7.7% in the fiscal year 2025-26, with growth accelerating to 7.8% in the final quarter. This performance reflects robust domestic demand and sustained industrial activity despite global economic headwinds.

India / market angle

This growth data reinforces India's position as a high-growth emerging market, likely supporting continued FII inflows and strengthening the INR. It also provides the RBI with more policy space to maintain interest rates if inflation remains within target, as the economy shows resilience without needing immediate stimulus.

What to watch

Upcoming RBI monetary policy committee meetings to see if the central bank adjusts its stance on interest rates in response to this growth data versus persistent inflation risks.

Trump intensifies pressure on Fed over interest rate policy

Global economy & central banks · CNBC · read original ↗

What happened

President-elect Donald Trump has renewed his public campaign to influence Federal Reserve policy, specifically targeting potential candidates like Kevin Warsh to push for lower interest rates. This comes as markets debate whether the Fed will hike rates in September, driven by concerns over labor market strength and inflation.

India / market angle

The Fed's interest rate trajectory is a primary determinant of global capital flows. If the Fed maintains higher rates due to political pressure or inflation, it risks strengthening the USD, which would put downward pressure on the INR and increase the cost of servicing India's dollar-denominated debt.

What to watch

The next Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting minutes and any official comments from Fed leadership regarding their independence from executive pressure.

US military strikes Iranian oil tankers

Global geopolitics · The Hindu · read original ↗

What happened

The U.S. military has conducted strikes against three Iranian oil tankers, marking a significant escalation in tensions between the two nations. This move follows ongoing regional instability and threatens to disrupt maritime oil transit routes in the Middle East.

India / market angle

As India imports a significant portion of its crude oil from the Middle East, any disruption to shipping lanes or a spike in global oil prices directly impacts India's import bill, widens the current account deficit, and risks stoking domestic fuel-led inflation.

What to watch

Brent crude oil price volatility and any updates on maritime security in the Strait of Hormuz, which is critical for India's energy imports.
