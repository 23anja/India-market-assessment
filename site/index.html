Strong October US Jobs Data Increases Probability of Federal Reserve Rate Hike

Global economy & central banks · Financial Times · read original ↗

What happened

Robust payroll data for October has signaled continued strength in the US labor market, leading market participants to anticipate a potential interest rate increase by the Federal Reserve in December. This data suggests that inflation remains persistent, forcing policymakers to maintain a hawkish stance despite previous expectations of a pause or pivot.

India / market angle

Higher US interest rates typically lead to a stronger USD and capital outflows from emerging markets like India as foreign institutional investors (FIIs) seek higher yields in US Treasuries. This puts downward pressure on the INR and complicates the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) monetary policy, as it must balance supporting the currency while managing domestic growth.

What to watch

The upcoming US Consumer Price Index (CPI) print and the subsequent Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting minutes for clear signals on the terminal interest rate.

India economy & policy · NDTV · read original ↗

What happened

A recent study indicates that air pollution is costing India up to 9.5% of its annual GDP, with millions of premature deaths linked to poor air quality. The report advocates for a shift toward health-led economic policies to mitigate these long-term productivity losses and healthcare burdens.

India / market angle

This represents a structural drag on India's long-term growth potential and fiscal health. It necessitates increased government spending on public health and green infrastructure, potentially shifting budget allocations away from other capital expenditures and impacting sectors like insurance, healthcare, and manufacturing productivity.

What to watch

Government policy announcements regarding stricter emission norms for industries and the implementation of national clean air programs in the upcoming Union Budget.

China Restricts Exports of Specialized Tunneling Machinery

China & global trade · The Straits Times · read original ↗

What happened

China has implemented restrictions on the export of advanced tunneling machinery, a move that affects global infrastructure projects reliant on Chinese equipment. This decision is part of a broader trend of China leveraging its manufacturing dominance to influence global supply chains.

India / market angle

India's massive infrastructure push, including metro rail and mountain tunnel projects, has historically relied on imported Chinese boring machines. These restrictions could lead to project delays, increased capital expenditure for Indian contractors, or the necessity to source more expensive alternatives from Europe or Japan, impacting the cost-benefit analysis of major infrastructure projects.

What to watch

Updates on procurement timelines for major Indian infrastructure projects and any potential government initiatives to incentivize domestic manufacturing of heavy tunneling equipment.
