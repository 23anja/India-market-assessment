What happened

The U.S. military has conducted strikes against three Iranian oil tankers in response to recent missile attacks, marking a significant escalation in regional hostilities. These actions appear to be a direct retaliation against Iranian maritime assets, further complicating the security situation in the Middle East. The move signals a more aggressive posture from the U.S. toward Iranian infrastructure.

India / market angle

India is highly sensitive to Middle Eastern instability due to its heavy reliance on imported crude oil. Any disruption to tanker traffic or a spike in global oil prices directly impacts India's import bill, widens the current account deficit, and exerts downward pressure on the INR. Furthermore, it threatens the security of key energy supply routes through the Strait of Hormuz.

What to watch

Monitor Brent crude oil price volatility and any official statements from the Indian Ministry of External Affairs regarding the safety of Indian-flagged vessels in the region.

Trump Pressures Fed for Rate Cuts Amid Market Uncertainty

Global economy & central banks · Livemint · read original ↗

What happened

President-elect Donald Trump has intensified his public campaign demanding that the Federal Reserve lower interest rates, specifically mentioning Kevin Warsh as a potential candidate for influence. While Trump seeks lower rates to stimulate growth, financial markets are currently pricing in a more cautious approach, reflecting concerns over potential inflation. This creates a significant test for the Fed's independence and its future policy direction.

India / market angle

The Fed's interest rate trajectory is the primary driver of global capital flows. If the Fed maintains higher rates due to inflation concerns, it exerts pressure on the RBI to keep Indian rates elevated to prevent capital flight and INR depreciation. Conversely, if Trump successfully forces a rate cut, it could trigger a surge in liquidity into emerging markets like India, boosting domestic equity valuations.

What to watch

Watch the upcoming Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting and any subsequent commentary from Fed officials regarding their policy stance in light of political pressure.
