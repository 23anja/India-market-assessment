What happened

The milestone underscores India's growing prominence in global capital allocation strategies.

Rare Earth Export Restrictions from China Cause Yttrium Price Spike

China & global trade · BigGo Finance · read original ↗

What happened

Yttrium prices have surged 174-fold following the implementation of new export controls by China on critical rare earth minerals. The move has disrupted global supply chains, particularly affecting industries in Japan and other manufacturing hubs that rely on these materials for high-tech components.

India / market angle

India is heavily dependent on imports for critical minerals needed for its growing electronics and green energy sectors. A global supply crunch increases input costs for Indian manufacturers and highlights the urgent strategic need to accelerate domestic mineral exploration and diversify supply chains away from China.

What to watch

Track the Ministry of Mines' policy updates regarding critical mineral auctions and any new trade agreements aimed at securing non-Chinese sources for rare earth elements.
