US Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady Amid Persistent Inflation

Global economy & central banks · ABC News - Breaking News, Latest News and Videos · read original ↗

What happened

The US Federal Reserve has maintained its current interest rate levels as recent data indicates inflation remains at a three-year high. Policymakers are balancing the need to cool price growth without triggering an economic downturn, leaving the door open for future adjustments depending on upcoming labor and consumer price reports.

India / market angle

The Fed's 'higher-for-longer' stance exerts pressure on the Indian Rupee (INR) by widening interest rate differentials, which can trigger capital outflows from Indian equities as foreign investors seek higher yields in US Treasuries. Additionally, it limits the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) room to maneuver on domestic rate cuts, as the RBI must maintain a spread to prevent excessive currency depreciation.

What to watch

The upcoming US Consumer Price Index (CPI) print and the next Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting minutes for signals on the timing of the first potential rate cut.

Global geopolitics · NDTV · read original ↗

What happened

Geopolitical instability in the Middle East has intensified as Iran issues strong warnings in response to potential US military actions. The rhetoric has escalated, with global markets closely monitoring the possibility of a direct confrontation that could disrupt regional security.

India / market angle

As a major importer of crude oil, India is highly sensitive to Middle Eastern conflict. A spike in regional tensions typically drives up global oil prices, which directly impacts India's import bill, widens the current account deficit, and puts upward pressure on domestic fuel prices and inflation.

What to watch

Any developments regarding the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil transit, and official statements from the Indian Ministry of External Affairs regarding regional stability.
