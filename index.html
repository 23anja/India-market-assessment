US Inflation Data Exceeds Expectations, Fueling Further Rate Hike Speculation

Global economy & central banks · Investopedia · read original ↗

What happened

Recent US economic data shows inflation remains stubborn, with October figures coming in hotter than market forecasts. This has led to renewed expectations that the Federal Reserve will maintain a hawkish stance, including potential further interest rate increases in December to cool demand. The persistence of these price pressures suggests that the 'last mile' of bringing inflation down to the 2% target is proving difficult.

India / market angle

For India, a hawkish Fed strengthens the US Dollar, which exerts downward pressure on the Indian Rupee (INR) and increases the cost of servicing dollar-denominated debt. Furthermore, persistent US rate hikes typically trigger capital outflows from emerging markets like India as foreign institutional investors (FIIs) shift capital toward higher-yielding, safer US Treasury assets, potentially creating volatility in the Nifty and Sensex.

What to watch

The upcoming Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting minutes and any subsequent commentary from Fed officials regarding the December rate decision.

China's Trade Surplus Surpasses $1 Trillion Amid Export Surge

China & global trade · ASHARQ AL-AWSAT English · read original ↗

What happened

China’s trade surplus has expanded significantly, crossing the $1 trillion mark, driven by a robust surge in exports, particularly in technology and AI-related hardware. While this highlights China's manufacturing dominance, analysts note that the export boom often masks underlying weaknesses in domestic consumption and internal economic growth. This imbalance has drawn scrutiny from global trading partners concerned about market flooding.

India / market angle

India faces a dual impact: while it relies on Chinese imports for critical components and raw materials, the surge in cheap Chinese exports can undercut domestic manufacturing efforts under the 'Make in India' initiative. Additionally, if China continues to rely on exports to sustain its economy, it may intensify dumping of manufactured goods into the Indian market, putting pressure on local sectors like steel, chemicals, and electronics.

What to watch

Future Chinese monthly trade data for signs of cooling in export growth and any potential anti-dumping investigations initiated by the Indian Ministry of Commerce.

Moody's Upgrades India's FY27 Growth Forecast to 7%

India economy & policy · Business Standard · read original ↗

What happened

Moody's Ratings has revised its growth forecast for India for the 2026-27 fiscal year to 7%, citing broad-based economic momentum. The agency highlights strong domestic demand, improvements in infrastructure, and a resilient services sector as primary drivers for this optimistic outlook. This upgrade reflects growing international confidence in India's medium-term macroeconomic stability.

India / market angle

This positive outlook acts as a signal to global investors, likely sustaining FII inflows into Indian equities and debt markets. It provides the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) with more flexibility in its monetary policy, as strong growth allows for a focus on inflation management without immediately sacrificing economic expansion, potentially keeping Indian sovereign bond yields attractive relative to other emerging markets.

What to watch

Upcoming quarterly GDP growth figures and high-frequency indicators like GST collections and manufacturing PMI to see if they align with the 7% trajectory.
